# A9 – Pulleys

## Objective

The objective of this assignment was to design a pulley system within given dimensions and parameters

## Analyze


The given within this assignment is that the Diameter of both pulleys is 3 in being 4-6 in apart. Choose rpm and Lbf of 1000-2000 rpm and 4-6 Lbf. Choose a material of ABS, Aluminum, or steel, and have a S.F. of 3.
<br>
We are given assumptions that the belt is a polyurethane rubber with a Sy of 3.25 MPa (471 psi) at 20% strain.
