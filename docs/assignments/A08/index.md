# A8 – Gears

## Objective
The objective of this assignment was to design a gearbox while analyzing the components to optimize their shape and size. We are then tasked to parametrically design a CAD model of this gearbox maintaining that it can be assembled and disassembled.
<br>

## Analyze

The spur gears are to have a 24:9 ratio and the output gear torque is 1.25 in-Lbf and 1100 rpm. The input (pinion,power-transmission) and output (gear, short shaft) shaft diameters are .125 and .25 inches respectively and have lengths of 1 inch.
<br>
### Shafts
 I first found the properties of the shafts starting with the power transmission. I found the velocity, torsion, torque, and power. For the power transmission, these values are all the inputs. For the short shaft, I had to find torsion and power as I already had the other outputs listed in the given.

## Design

I first started with making the gearbox main body. This was done by taking the Pitch diameter and creating two circles side-by-side, adjusting the diameter for each part by then creating enough space around the gears and creating the wall, after this I extruded and fillet for looks. After doing this, I then created the holes for the screws, which have a diameter of 0.06 in.
